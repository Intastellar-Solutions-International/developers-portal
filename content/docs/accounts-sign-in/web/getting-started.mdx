This guide orients you for **web** sign-in with Intastellar Accounts. For every parameter, callback URL, and SDK method, use the hosted reference below.

## Before you integrate

1. **Register your application** in the Intastellar developer console (redirect URIs, client configuration).
2. **Choose your flow** — typically authorization code with PKCE for public clients, or a confidential server-side exchange where applicable.
3. **Handle sessions** — store tokens securely (httpOnly cookies or server-side session); avoid exposing long-lived secrets in the browser.
