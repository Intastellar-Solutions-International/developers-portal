# Config (WordPress)

In the WordPress plugin, complete the **Intro** and **Privacy Policy** sections with valid URLs and copy. A missing or invalid privacy policy is a common reason the banner does not appear.

If you have integrated our cookie banner via WordPress, make sure that you have provided a valid privacy policy under the section privacy policy or in the intro section.

You can add a link to your privacy policy via WordPress under the section **Intro** or **Privacy Policy** (see also the [documentation home](/docs/cookie-banner)).
