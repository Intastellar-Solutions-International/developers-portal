## Google Tagmanager

Integrate our solutions on your Website via Google Tagmanager.

You are using Google Tagmanager and want to integrate our solution? Great with our Google Tagmanager Solution. Load the scripts from the [JavaScript Solution](/docs/cookie-banner/javascript/getting-started) via a Custom HTML-Tag. Use **Consent Initialization - All pages** as trigger in GTM to initialize the banner. After you´ve config it, you can add new tags and triggers. We recommend to only fire the other tags, after our solution has fired.

[Get started](/docs/cookie-banner/google-tag-manager)

### Can I use my own design

Yes, it is possible to integrate our solution with your custom design. You just need to implement our script, and add a few css classes to you banner.

### I have trouble to get the cookie banner to show up on my site - what should I do?

If you have integrated our cookie banner via WordPress, make sure that you have provided a valid privacy policy under the section privacy policy or in the intro section. Read more under [Config](/docs/cookie-banner/wordpress/config).

If you have integrated via JavaScript check if your privacy policy contains under settings a valid privacy url. (See more under [JavaScript docs](/docs/cookie-banner/javascript/getting-started).)

### Where do I add a link to my privacy policy

You can add a link to your privacy policy under `settings.privacy_policy` in the `window.INTA` object or via WordPress under the section "Intro" or "Privacy Policy".
